## Three expectations

**Respect yourself.** Complete assignments. Give your best effort to learn. Practice English through listening, speaking, reading and writing. Ask for a break when you need it.

**Respect others.** Be kind and supportive. Talk and listen at appropriate times. Ask for help when you need it. Follow the teachers’ instructions.

**Respect our school.** Follow schoolwide expectations. Be a leader in the hallways and restrooms. No digital devices during the day. Clean up after yourself.
