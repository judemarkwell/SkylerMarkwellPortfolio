## What each probe checks

**Newcomer:** personal information, today's and yesterday's date, weather and temperature, numbers to 100, classroom objects, shapes, rooms of a house, clothing, feelings, counting.

**Beginner:** community places, jobs in the community, transportation, road signs, foods, comparing sizes and heights, animals, the United States, everyday activities.
