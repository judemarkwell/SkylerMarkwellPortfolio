## Free book sources it recommends

- Starfall, for word-building and phonemic awareness
- Storyplace Library, for themed low-level reading
- Epic! Books, with filters by level
- The district library site (MackinVia), including bilingual and Spanish books
- ABRACADABRA, for its reading section

Tips are adapted from *The ELL Teacher’s Toolbox* by Larry Ferlazzo and Katie Hull Sypnieski.
