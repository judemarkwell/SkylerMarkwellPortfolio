## What students do

1. Draw a scene Columbus would use to show himself, using at least one excerpt, and explain it in one sentence.
2. Draw a scene de las Casas would use to show Columbus, and explain it.
3. Draw a scene that shows whether they think Columbus was good or bad, and explain it.

## What the rubric looks for

Four criteria, each scored from 1 (not evident) to 4 (exemplary), for 16 points in all.

- Defends Columbus's view with examples from the excerpts
- Defends de las Casas's view with examples from the excerpts
- States a personal opinion backed by the excerpts
- Tells the same story in the drawing and the sentence
