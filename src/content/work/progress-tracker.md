## What it tracks

- **Reading:** sight words and CVC words (DIBELS WRF and NWF), reading accuracy (DIBELS ORF), main idea, literal and inferential questions, retelling in sequence
- **Writing:** paragraph mechanics, simple sentences, COPS conventions
- **Behavior and self-advocacy:** asking for help, focus, self-regulation, self-responsibility, completing assignments, decision-making

## How it works

1. Each week, the plan assigns every student two goals by class period.
2. Every quiz, writing sample or project gets logged against the goal with a rubric score, a percentage and a short note.
3. The logs show growth from baseline and where to change the support.
