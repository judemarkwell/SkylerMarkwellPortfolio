## Criteria

1. **Central idea and restatement** (RACE R and A): restates the prompt in a complete sentence and answers it directly.
2. **Text evidence and citation** (RACE C and E): cites specific evidence with a citation phrase and explains how it supports the answer.
3. **Conventions and mechanics** (COPS): capitals, organization, punctuation and spelling.

Each criterion is scored Exceeds (4), Meets (3), Developing (2), Emerging (1) or no response (0).
