## Warm-ups

Students pick one exercise to practice while taking deep breaths.

<ul class="cols">
<li>Somatic breathing</li><li>Somatic shakeout</li><li>Shoulder shrug</li><li>Finger stretch</li><li>Body scan</li>
<li>Box breathing</li><li>Speaking with one voice</li><li>Guided meditation</li><li>Body stretch</li><li>Affirmations</li>
</ul>

## Why the circle exists

- Speak in English with each other
- Learn how to solve problems in a group
- Practice respect, curiosity and self-love
- Learn about ourselves and others
