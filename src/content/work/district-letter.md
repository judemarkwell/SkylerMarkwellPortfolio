## What the letter asks for

**The problem of practice.** Staff named school vision, especially low enrollment, as the main concern, and asked for a leader who will retain and recruit both staff and students.

**Leadership competencies staff asked the committee to prioritize:**

- Instructional leadership and academic rigor
- Cultural responsiveness and community engagement with immigrant and refugee families
- Operational expertise and post-secondary pathways
- Holistic, whole-child student support
- Resource optimization and professional growth
- Culture, climate and strategic positioning

**A performance task for candidates:** a written justification that they bring these qualities, and a proposal for addressing the school’s problem of practice.
