## The five skills

- Productive Collaborator: someone who works hard in a group
- Emerging Innovator: someone coming up with new ideas
- Globally and Culturally Competent Citizen: someone who understands that their city and world have different cultures
- Prepared and Resilient Learner: someone who is ready to keep going and learning when it’s hard
- Effective Communicator: someone who is good at talking and solving problems

Each row ends with the same stem: *I am a ____ because I can… In this project, I…*
