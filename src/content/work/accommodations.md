## On a test, I can have…

The Spanish page explains each support the same way.

<ul class="plain">
<li>Extended time: you get extra time to finish your test.</li>
<li>Reader: your buddy or a computer reads your test out loud.</li>
<li>Oral native language support: you can hear the test in your native language.</li>
<li>Dictionary: you can look up key words in your native language.</li>
<li>Scribe: your buddy writes what you say.</li>
</ul>
