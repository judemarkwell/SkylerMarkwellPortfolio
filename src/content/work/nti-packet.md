## The six choices

The Spanish page gives the same six choices.

<ul class="plain">
<li>Watch a show, a movie, or listen to a song in English. Write a summary in your first language.</li>
<li>Write a story in English. Try to translate it into your first language when you finish.</li>
<li>Draw a picture and explain what’s happening in the picture.</li>
<li>Read a book and write 3 words you learned.</li>
<li>Teach someone a new word. Tell who you taught it to and what the word means.</li>
<li>Pick a controversial topic and explain your side.</li>
</ul>
