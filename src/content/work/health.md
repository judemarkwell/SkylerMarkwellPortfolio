## Seven units, one skill each

Each unit pairs a health skill with a topic students meet in their first year in the United States, then moves through the skills-based model in five steps.

## Unit 1 guiding questions

1. What are ways that wellness is measured?
2. What are ways that my culture values wellness?
3. How does my background affect the decisions I make with my wellness?
4. What are the inequities that prevent me from accessing wellness?
5. How do others, through media or otherwise, change my health choices?
6. How can my genetics influence the decisions I make and my overall well-being?
