# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Tailwind CSS, static output, deployed to GitHub Pages from this repo via GitHub Actions. Each portfolio piece is a content-collection entry so new work can be added as a file.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Instructional design / L&D hiring managers** evaluating whether a classroom teacher can design learning at scale. They want process, structure, outcomes, and evidence of designing for specific learners.
- **School and district leaders** hiring for multilingual-learner, newcomer, or program-lead roles. They want classroom practice, family engagement, compliance fluency, and student support.

Both arrive from a resume or LinkedIn link, skim the homepage, and open two or three pieces. Skyler intends to move toward instructional design over time, but the site must serve both audiences now.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio for Skyler Markwell, a teacher of multilingual learners and curriculum designer in Louisville, Kentucky. It shows real materials she made, why she made them, and who they served, so a reader can judge her design ability from the work itself rather than from resume claims.

Success: a reader from either audience can open any piece and, within a minute, understand the learner, the problem, what she built, and how it works.

## Positioning

Her materials are designed for learners who are still acquiring English: picture supports, bilingual and trilingual versions, sentence frames, scaffolded assessment. Designing for the hardest-to-reach learner is the claim, and the work is the proof. Each piece is presented so both audiences read it in their own terms (a lesson is also a learning experience; a family FAQ is also stakeholder communication).

## Operating Context

- Her materials are mostly paper or print-first: handouts, posters, rubrics, laminated cards, slide decks, a scope and sequence spreadsheet. The gallery presents them as sheets on a desk that can be picked up (prototype: `prototypes/desk/`).
- Her own visual style in those materials is whimsical and vibrant (flowing color shapes, pastel doodles, bright picture supports).
- Readers mostly arrive on a laptop from a job application, sometimes on a phone from LinkedIn.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Every page shows a real artifact before it describes one.
2. Write each piece for both readers: the classroom context and the design decision, in plain language.
3. Show the learner. Every piece names who it was for and what they needed.
4. Never invent. Her reasons, outcomes, and quotes come from her.
5. Protect students and colleagues. No student names, faces, or records; check every file before it goes public.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every interaction (including the desk pick-up) works with keyboard and screen reader and respects reduced motion. Document previews always come with real text. Bilingual content is marked with the correct `lang`.
