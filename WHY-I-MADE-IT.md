# "Why I made it" notes

Each work page shows a "Why I made it" note once Skyler writes one. A couple of sentences in her own words is plenty.
To add one, put a `why:` line in that piece's file, under its `prompt:` line, for example:

```
why: "My students needed a way to ask for a break before they had the English words for it."
```
